Stovetop

Chicken Tortilla Soup

One pot
  1. Heat chicken stock in a pot, stir in enchilada sauce.
  2. Add shredded chicken verde and frozen corn, simmer until heated through.
  3. Top with shredded cheese and sliced avocado.

Pasta and Meatballs

One pot
  1. Boil pasta until al dente, drain.
  2. In the same pot, sauté diced onion and zucchini briefly, then add marinara sauce and frozen meatballs.
  3. Simmer until meatballs are heated through, combine with pasta.

Fried Rice

One skillet
  1. Heat frozen fried rice in a skillet per package directions.
  2. Push rice aside, scramble an egg in the same pan, mix in.
  3. Stir in frozen edamame until heated through.

Jambalaya

One skillet
  1. Sauté sliced sausage and diced bell pepper in a skillet until browned.
  2. Add frozen jambalaya, heat through per package directions, stirring occasionally.

Microwave

Orange Chicken Stir Fry

Microwave
  1. Microwave frozen orange chicken per package directions.
  2. Microwave frozen stir-fry veggies and frozen rice separately.
  3. Combine all together and serve.

Oven

Chicken Enchiladas

One tray
  1. Fill tortillas with chicken verde, frozen corn, and shredded cheese, roll and place in a baking dish.
  2. Top with enchilada sauce and more cheese.
  3. Bake until bubbly and cheese is melted; top with sliced avocado.

Pizza and Salad

One tray
  1. Bake frozen pizza straight from freezer per package directions.
  2. Serve with bagged salad kit on the side. No extra dishes.

Lasagna and Salad

One tray
  1. Bake ready-made lasagna per package directions.
  2. Serve with bagged salad kit and dinner rolls.
